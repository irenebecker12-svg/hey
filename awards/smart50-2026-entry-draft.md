## Business snapshot (overview / description field)

99aupairs is Australia's best-rated au pair agency, founded in Glen Eira in 2017. We match Australian families with carefully screened, internationally-sourced live-in au pairs — and, uniquely in Australia, we stand behind every placement with a **12-month continuity guarantee**: unlimited rematches at no extra placement fee, and continuity of care between au pairs.

We are a **female-owned, family-owned team of five** (four of us women) serving families nationally. In nine years we have supported **more than 1,000 families** — an estimated two million hours of in-home care — at a **4.9-star rating across 190 Google reviews**, with around **40% of families arriving by referral**. In the past 12 months we grew revenue by **50%**, and our core au pair business by **89%**, with the team still at five.

The engine behind that scale is **proprietary AI we build ourselves** — MAIA, ANNA and DONNA — which lets a five-person company deliver what once required a large agency. Our mission is to support **10,000 families by 2030**, now extending through **99supports** (families raising children with additional needs) and **Aceso Care** (helping older Australians remain safely at home).

We don't provide childcare. We give families the confidence to thrive.

### Q. What has driven your revenue growth?
Three things compounded. **A service promise no competitor matches** — our 12-month guarantee with unlimited rematches turns anxious first-time enquirers into long-term, referring customers; roughly 40% of new families now come by referral. **Technology that removes the cost ceiling on growth** — MAIA (asynchronous global interviewing), ANNA (recruitment operations) and DONNA (after-hours AI voice) automate the admin that throttles traditional agencies, so we grow **+50%** on a team of five without adding headcount; international recruitment that once took 2–3 weeks now moves from application to offer in ~24 hours. **Demand we create** — our free public Childcare Cost Calculator brings families into our orbit at the moment they first weigh their options, feeding a pipeline we convert with service, not sales pressure.

### Q. What makes your business stand out?
We're the only Australian agency that guarantees a placement for a **full 12 months** with unlimited rematches — we don't just make a match, we stand behind it. Under that promise sits Australian-owned AI we design in-house, which is why five people can serve 1,000+ families nationally at 4.9 stars. Competitors are either high-touch boutiques that can't scale, or low-touch marketplaces that don't stand behind the match. We do both — and our 4.9-star rating held right through the rebuild.

### Q. What are your plans for growth?
Reach **10,000 families by 2030**. Two adjacent verticals extend the same trusted-in-home-care model to large, underserved markets: **99supports** for families raising children with additional needs, and **Aceso Care** helping older Australians stay safely at home. Each reuses our matching technology, vetting rigour and continuity guarantee, so growth compounds on infrastructure we've already built.

### Q. Tell us about the founder (Founder of the Year category)
Irene Becker founded 99aupairs from lived experience — migrating to Australia with young children and no family nearby, building a career while raising them, until an au pair changed everything. She started the company in 2017 so other families (and, disproportionately, other women) wouldn't have to choose, funding the early years by working full-time and putting service quality ahead of revenue. She was named among **Inside Small Business's Top 50 Small Business Leaders** and returned the following year as a **judge**, and won **Bronze at the 2019 AusMumpreneur Awards**. Nine years on she leads a five-person, majority-women team supporting 1,000+ families, with revenue up 50% in the last year and two new care verticals underway.

### Q. Social / community impact
We measure ourselves by the careers we keep alive — a pilot still flying, a nurse still in the role her community needs, a lawyer on the partnership track instead of stepping off it. These families didn't need childcare; they needed care that flexes around shift work, early starts and unpredictable rosters — exactly what centre-based care can't do. Our free Childcare Cost Calculator gives **any** Australian family a clear, subsidy-inclusive answer to what care actually costs (updated the week the Government changed the rates in July 2026), whether or not they ever become a customer. Our new 99supports and Aceso Care verticals extend trusted in-home care to families with additional needs and to ageing Australians.
