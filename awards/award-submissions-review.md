Your three 2026 AusMumpreneur entries — Women's Champion, Parenting & Family Services and Digital Innovation, all submitted **July 2026** — each state **estimated annual turnover (last 12 months) of $100k–$300k**, with **+50%** growth. **Recommendation:** before paying the $200 + GST entry fee, confirm actual turnover against Xero / your accountant.
- If real turnover **is above $500k** (e.g. the AusMumpreneur band was a conservative round-down), Smart50 is worth entering — the draft in `awards/smart50-2026-entry-draft.md` is ready to populate.

The awards you're already entering (AusMumpreneur, Telstra Best of Business, Glen Eira) fit far better, and you can revisit Smart50 once turnover clears the threshold. Don't spend the entry fee on an entry that fails eligibility verification.
