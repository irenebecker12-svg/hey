The winnable battle

How to actually get cited in AI Overviews

Ranking #1 and being quoted by Google's AI are now two different algorithms. Five tactics that move the second one — apply to every page you publish.

1

Answer in the first 60 words

Open each page with a direct, 40–60 word answer to the exact question — no "Welcome to our guide". This is the block AI lifts verbatim.

2

Phrase headings as questions

H2s written as the real query ("How much does an au pair cost in Australia?") map cleanly to how people and AI ask.

3

Make facts extractable

Costs, rules and comparisons in tables and lists, not paragraphs — structured facts are what AI pulls into an Overview.

4

Be one consistent entity

Same name, address and sameAs links (LinkedIn, Mamamia, CAPAA) everywhere, so Google resolves 99aupairs and Irene as trusted entities.

5

Get validated off-site

The biggest lever, and the one the original plan under-weights: AI Overviews cite authoritative third-party mentions. The data report is your engine for earning them.

Honest review of the ChatGPT plan

What to keep, what to cut, what to fix

Keep — it's right
  • AI-Overview as a separate, winnable battle
  • Topic clusters over standalone pages
  • Answer-first structure for every page
  • "Become the source everyone quotes" via original data
  • Reposition around family care decisions, not just "au pair"
Cut — vanity at this stage
  • 100+ pages in 90 days → dilutes quality & internal links
  • 100 YouTube videos → 12 great ones out-perform
  • "300 reviews" → chase genuine velocity, not a number
  • 20+ tools → 3 excellent tools beat 20 half-built ones
Fix — the missing weight
  • Plan over-indexes on your own page volume
  • Under-weights third-party citations — the real AI lever
  • No sequencing: publish existing drafts before writing new
  • Add schema as a day-one requirement, not "Phase 11"

The one strategic shift worth making: the plan's best idea is buried at the end — stop positioning as an au pair agency and become Australia's authority on modern family-care decisions. Everything you've built (the calculator, MAIA, Donna, the research, the awards, Irene's story) already supports it, and it expands your footprint from a few thousand "au pair" searches to hundreds of thousands around childcare cost, subsidy, return-to-work and nanny comparisons.

That breadth of genuine expertise is exactly what AI Overviews reward — and it's a moat AIFS, Dream Nannies and JCR can't quickly copy.
