# Au Pairs in Melbourne

> **Quick answer.** Melbourne families hire au pairs for flexible, live-in childcare that
> works around commutes, split shifts and school runs across the city's suburbs. An au pair
> lives with your family and provides in-home care in exchange for pocket money, board and
> meals. 99aupairs matches, screens and supports Melbourne placements end to end.

## Why Melbourne families choose an au pair

- **Commute-proof care** — early starts and late finishes across Melbourne's spread-out suburbs.
- **School-holiday cover** — continuous care through Victorian term breaks.
- **Multiple children** — one flat cost instead of per-child daycare fees.
- **Cultural exchange** — a live-in relationship, not just a service.

## What does an au pair cost in Melbourne?

The core cost is weekly pocket money plus board and meals, with an agency fee for screening,
matching and support. Melbourne daycare fees vary by suburb and are reduced by CCS; an au pair
is a flat cost that doesn't rise with each child. Compare after-subsidy daycare with all-in au
pair cost using the [Childcare Calculator](/childcare-calculator). `[VERIFY: local figures]`
