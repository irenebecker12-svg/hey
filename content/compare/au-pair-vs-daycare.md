# Au Pair vs Daycare in Australia

> **Quick answer.** Daycare is a licensed centre-based service that attracts the Child
> Care Subsidy, with fixed operating hours. An au pair is a live-in helper who gives
> flexible, one-on-one in-home care but is not CCS-eligible. For families with several
> children, irregular hours, or a regional/shift-work lifestyle, an au pair is often
> cheaper all-in and far more flexible; for a single child in standard hours, subsidised
> daycare can be very competitive.

## At a glance

| | Au pair | Daycare |
|---|---|---|
| Setting | Your home, one-on-one | Licensed centre, group |
| Hours | Flexible — you set them | Fixed centre hours |
| CCS subsidy | No | Yes (income-tested) |
| Cost basis | Pocket money + board (flat) | Per-child daily fee, minus CCS |
| Scales with kids? | Flat — great for 2+ children | Per-child — rises with each child |
| Best for | Flexible/regional/shift families, 2+ kids | Single child, standard hours, socialisation |

## Is an au pair cheaper than daycare?

It depends on your children and hours. Daycare fees are **per child** and reduced by CCS,
so for one child in standard hours daycare can be competitive. An au pair is a **flat**
cost regardless of the number of children, so for two or more kids — or long/irregular
hours — an au pair is often cheaper overall. Compare *after-subsidy* daycare against
*all-in* au pair cost with the [Childcare Calculator](/childcare-calculator).

## The subsidy difference that matters

Because daycare attracts the Child Care Subsidy and an au pair does not, always compare
your **after-CCS** daycare fee — not the sticker price — against the au pair's all-in cost.
Our calculator uses current government CCS rules to do this accurately.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does an au pair qualify for the Child Care Subsidy?**
No. Only approved services such as licensed daycare attract CCS.

**Is an au pair cheaper than daycare for two children?**
Often yes — an au pair is a flat cost while daycare charges per child.

**Can I use both?**
Yes — many families combine daycare days with an au pair for wrap-around flexibility.
