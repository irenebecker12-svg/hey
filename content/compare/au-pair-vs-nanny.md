# Au Pair vs Nanny in Australia

> **Quick answer.** An au pair is a live-in overseas helper who provides childcare
> and light household help in exchange for pocket money, board and meals as part of a
> cultural exchange. A nanny is usually a live-out childcare professional paid an award
> wage. Au pairs cost less and offer live-in flexibility; nannies bring formal training
> and set professional hours. Neither is eligible for the Child Care Subsidy.

## Which is cheaper — an au pair or a nanny?

An au pair is generally the more affordable option, because the core cost is weekly
pocket money plus board rather than an hourly professional wage. A nanny costs more per
hour but requires no live-in space and brings professional experience. For total
out-of-pocket cost across your real weekly schedule, use the
[Childcare Calculator](/childcare-calculator).

## When a nanny is the better choice

Choose a nanny if you need a qualified professional, have no room for a live-in helper,
prefer fixed hours, or your child has specific needs that call for trained experience.

## When an au pair is the better choice

Choose an au pair for flexible, wrap-around in-home care — irregular hours, several
children, school holidays, regional locations, or shift-working and FIFO households —
and for the cultural relationship a live-in brings.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is an au pair cheaper than a nanny in Australia?**
Usually yes — the main au pair cost is pocket money plus board, versus a nanny's hourly award wage.

**Can an au pair do everything a nanny does?**
Au pairs focus on children and light related help; nannies often bring more formal training.

**Do either qualify for CCS?**
No — neither au pairs nor private nannies are CCS-approved providers.
