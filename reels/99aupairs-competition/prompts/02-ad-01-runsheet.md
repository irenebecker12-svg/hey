## STEP 0 — Hero still (image, not video)

Iterate on this one image until both faces are right. It becomes the start frame / character
reference for every clip with a visible face. Aspect 9:16 or 2:3.

```
Candid documentary photograph of two young women standing close together on a sunlit
Australian beach, mid-laugh, looking at each other rather than the camera.

LEFT — 24, Spanish. Warm olive skin with visible pores and sun freckles across the nose.
Dark brown wavy shoulder-length hair, salt-damp and imperfect. Brown eyes, thick natural
brows, a small gap between her front teeth. 168cm, athletic-slim. Small gold hoop earrings,
thin white shell necklace. Rust-orange bikini.

RIGHT — 22, German. Fair skin, sun-pink across the shoulders and nose, light freckles.
Dirty-blonde long straight hair falling out of a messy bun. Blue-grey eyes, pale lashes.
175cm, noticeably taller than the woman on the left. Slim. Small silver nose stud, faded
red string bracelet on her left wrist. Sage-green bikini.

Bright midday sun, turquoise ocean and white sand behind them. Both faces fully visible
and sharp. Shot on Sony FX3, 35mm, shallow depth of field, natural sunlight, documentary
realism, natural imperfect skin texture, film grain, true-to-life colour.
```

**Negative:**
```
text, watermark, logo, distorted hands, extra fingers, missing fingers, deformed face,
```
