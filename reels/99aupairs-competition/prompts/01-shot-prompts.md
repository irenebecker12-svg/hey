# Shot Prompts — 9 generations, 18 seconds

Generate at **5s each** even where the timeline only uses 2s. You want the handles for
cutting on the beat, and you want the option to pick the best 2 seconds out of 5.

Every prompt below is **motion + subject only**. Before you submit:
- append the **style suffix** from `00-character-bible.md`
- paste the **negative prompt** from `00-character-bible.md`
- set aspect **9:16**, and the longest duration your plan allows

**Camera notes** are written in plain English first. If your Higgsfield plan exposes DoP
camera-motion presets, the bracketed name is the closest match — check what your account
actually offers rather than trusting the label, since preset names change between releases.

---

## SHOT 1 — Beach sprint · uses 0.00–2.00 · THE SCROLL-STOPPER

> Two young women in bikinis sprinting hand in hand across blinding white sand toward
> turquoise ocean, seen from behind, long hair flying, wet sand spraying up from their heels,
> they are laughing and pulling each other forward. Camera races low and fast behind them at
> ankle height, then lifts and climbs over their heads out across the open water. Harsh
> midday Australian sun, strong lens flare, deep blue horizon.

**Camera:** low fast chase → rise over water. [FPV Drone / Crash Zoom Out]
**Start frame:** rear-view still of Maya + Freya on the sand — faces not visible, so this is
the most forgiving shot to generate. Prioritise the *motion*, not the likeness.
**Selection note:** the first 6 frames decide whether the reel works. Pick the take where
sand spray hits the lens.

---

## SHOT 2A — Underwater turtle · uses 2.00–4.00

> Two young women snorkelling in crystal-clear shallow tropical water, filmed from below.
> A large green sea turtle glides slowly between them, close to camera. Sunbeams cut through
> the surface above in moving shafts, coral garden on the sand floor below. The women hang
> weightless, one reaches a slow hand toward the turtle. Bubbles drift upward.

**Camera:** slow drifting rise from the sea floor, GoPro-style wide. [Dolly In / Underwater]
**Start frame:** snorkel still — masks cover most of the face, which hides identity drift.
**Selection note:** reject any take where the turtle's flippers morph or duplicate.

---

## SHOT 2B — Surface scream · uses 4.00–5.00

> Two young women burst up through the surface of turquoise water, rip their snorkel masks
> off and scream with delight at each other, water streaming down their faces and hair,
> mask marks on their cheeks, absolutely elated, one grabs the other's shoulder.
> Handheld camera at water level, water droplets on the lens.

**Camera:** handheld, water level, slight shake. [Handheld / Snorricam]
**Start frame:** REQUIRED — full faces are visible here. Use your hero still.
**Selection note:** this is the emotional peak of the first half. Real joy or regenerate.

---

## SHOT 3 — Sydney Harbour golden hour · uses 5.00–8.00

> Two young women in summer dresses laughing and dancing on the open deck of a boat on
> Sydney Harbour, glasses raised, hair moving in the wind. The Sydney Opera House and the
> Sydney Harbour Bridge are clearly visible behind them. Other young people celebrating,
> soft and out of focus in the background. Warm golden-hour light, sun low and behind them,
> rim light on their hair.

**Camera:** smooth orbit around the pair, roughly 90°. [360 Orbit / Robo Arm]
**Start frame:** REQUIRED — sundress wardrobe still.
**Selection note:** landmarks are the #1 failure here. Reject bent Opera House shells,
doubled bridges, or a bridge that melts on the orbit. If it will not hold, shoot the orbit
tighter so the skyline reads only as a soft background suggestion.

---

## SHOT 4A — Penguin parade · uses 8.00–9.60

> Tiny little penguins waddling in a loose group up a dark wet beach from the ocean at dusk,
> Phillip Island. Deep blue twilight, last band of orange light on the horizon, wet sand
> reflecting the sky. Low camera at sand level, penguins moving toward and past the lens.

**Camera:** static low angle, very slight push. [Object POV / Low Angle]
**Start frame:** not required — no faces. Pure text-to-video is fine.
**Selection note:** little penguins are SMALL — about 33cm. Prompt "tiny" hard, and reject
any take that generates emperor or king penguins. That single error will read as fake to
every Australian who sees it.

---

## SHOT 4B — The quiet reaction · uses 9.60–11.00

> Two young women sitting close together on a dark beach at dusk wrapped in oversized
> jackets, watching something off-camera with delight. They turn to look at each other and
> share a quiet astonished smile, one covers her mouth in awe. Deep blue twilight, soft
> orange residual light on their faces, no harsh light source.

**Camera:** slow push in, handheld. [Dolly In / Handheld]
**Start frame:** REQUIRED — jacket wardrobe still.
**Selection note:** this shot is the emotional breath before the CTA. Quiet, not performed.

---

## SHOT 5D — Coastal lookout · used in montage 11.00–13.00

> Two young women standing on a windy coastal lookout above the ocean, laughing as their
> hair whips completely across their faces, trying to hold it back and failing, grabbing each
> other. Vast blue Australian coastline behind. Bright natural daylight.

**Camera:** handheld, slight whip pan onto them. [Whip Pan / Handheld]
**Start frame:** REQUIRED — denim/khaki wardrobe still.
**Note:** the montage reuses 2A, 3 and 4A. This is the only *new* clip the montage needs.

---

## SHOT 6 — Best friends · uses 13.00–15.00

> Two young women laughing hard together, arms wrapped around each other's shoulders,
> foreheads nearly touching, one doubled over laughing. Golden late-afternoon sun, warm rim
> light. Completely candid, unposed, they are not looking at the camera.

**Camera:** handheld, slow drift in. [Handheld / Dolly In]
**Start frame:** REQUIRED — same wardrobe as 5D and 7 (continuous scene).

---

## SHOT 7 — Clifftop CTA · uses 15.00–18.00 · LEAVE THE SKY EMPTY

> Wide shot of two young women standing side by side on a grassy clifftop at golden hour,
> overlooking a vast Australian coastline and ocean far below. They turn toward the camera
> and laugh. They are positioned in the LOWER THIRD of the frame. Enormous open golden sky
> fills the upper half of the frame, empty, no clouds in the centre.

**Camera:** slow drone pull back and rise. [FPV Drone / Crash Zoom Out]
**Start frame:** REQUIRED.
**Selection note:** compositionally this is the most demanding shot — you need genuinely
empty sky in the top third for the logo lockup and room at the bottom for terms. If the
generator keeps centring them, generate wider and reframe in the edit.

---

## Generation order

Do them in this order — it front-loads the shots that are hardest to save later:

`3 → 7 → 2B → 6 → 4B → 5D → 1 → 2A → 4A`

Shots 3 and 7 carry landmark and composition risk; shot 1 and 4A are nearly foolproof.
